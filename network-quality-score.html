Network quality guide

What Does a Network Quality Score Mean?

A network quality score is a summary of connection signals. It can help you read a result quickly, but it should not replace the raw measurements. Check the score together with download speed, upload speed, ping, jitter, packet loss, server, connection type, and test time.

What the score can help you see

Bandwidth

Download and upload results describe how much data the connection can move during the test.

Responsiveness

Ping and latency describe how quickly the connection responds to a test server.

Stability

Jitter and packet loss help explain inconsistent timing or missing data.

How to use the score responsibly

  1. 1. Read the raw values. A score is easier to interpret when you can see the measurements behind it.
  2. 2. Check the test context. Server, Wi-Fi or mobile data, device, VPN, background traffic, and time can affect results.
  3. 3. Compare repeated tests. Patterns are more useful than one isolated score.
  4. 4. Match the next step to the symptom. Use ping for delay, jitter for inconsistent delay, and packet loss for missing data.

What it cannot prove

  • It does not guarantee every app or game will feel the same.
  • It does not prove an ISP is throttling traffic.
  • It does not replace testing the room or location where the problem occurs.
  • It does not measure a video service by playing that service.

Frequently asked questions

What does a network quality score summarize?

A network quality score is a summary of measured connection signals such as speed, latency, jitter, and packet loss. The exact interpretation depends on the app's scoring model and test conditions.

Is a high network score a guarantee?

No. A score summarizes a test at a particular time and server. It does not guarantee that every app, game, video service, room, or time of day will perform the same way.

Should I still read the raw metrics?

Yes. Read the score together with download speed, upload speed, ping, jitter, packet loss, server, connection type, and test time.
